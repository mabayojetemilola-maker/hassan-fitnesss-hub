The coach

Hassan’s story

Hassan wasn’t always strong. As a boy he had a weak physique and was bullied in primary school. Growing up, he often felt alone — lonely, with nothing that made people look twice.

He tried to change that. He started with dance and flexible twist-outs, hoping to earn an audience. The attention never really came. Still, the hunger to become someone people noticed stayed.

About ten months ago, alone at home with nothing to do, he started training. No gym. No fancy setup. Just calisthenics — day after day on the floor and on parallettes. Within the first stretch of months he unlocked the elbow lever, the L-sit, and kept pushing. Progress stacked: ninety-degree work, cleaner handstands, harder holds.

Around four months ago he took it to TikTok — starting from zero followers. He posts under @ibn__walid (Khalid ibn al-Walid). Right now: 1,139 followers and 28.7K likes. The skills you see here come from that stretch: consistency when nobody was watching.

Now he coaches others who want the same path — not overnight magic, but steady skill work: handstand, planche, V-sit, hollow back, and the strength under them.
